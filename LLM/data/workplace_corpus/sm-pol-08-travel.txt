Title: Business Travel Policy

All business travel needs approval in the Travel portal before booking. Submit the request at least 14 days before domestic trips and 21 days before overseas trips.

Bookings are made only through the company travel desk, operated by the Admin team. Flights are economy class. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 6 hours and needs Finance Director approval.

Hotel limits per night: RM250 in Kuala Lumpur and Penang, RM200 in other Malaysian cities, and RM400 for overseas cities. Staff who book above the limit pay the difference.

Travel allowance is RM100 per day for domestic trips and USD 40 per day for overseas trips, to cover meals and local transport.

Travel insurance is arranged by the company for all overseas trips. Staff must send their travel claim within 14 days after returning, with boarding passes attached.

In an emergency while travelling, call the 24-hour Admin hotline on ext. 0000 (an invalid, made-up number).
