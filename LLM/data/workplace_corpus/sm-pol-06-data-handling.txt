Title: Data Handling Policy

Seri Maju Berhad classifies data into 3 levels: Public, Internal, and Confidential. Customer personal data and salary records are Confidential.

Rules for Confidential data:
- Store it only on approved company drives. Never on personal USB sticks or personal cloud accounts.
- Share it only with people who need it for their job.
- Encrypt it when sending outside the company.

Customer personal data is kept for 7 years after the contract ends, then deleted by the Data Protection Officer (DPO) team within 60 days.

If you suspect a data breach, report it to the DPO at dpo@serimaju.example within 24 hours. The DPO decides if regulators must be told. Do not try to investigate on your own.

Do not paste Confidential data into public AI chat tools. Only tools approved by IT Security may be used for company data.

All staff complete a short data handling refresher every year.
