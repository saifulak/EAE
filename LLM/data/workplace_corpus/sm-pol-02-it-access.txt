Title: IT Access and Password FAQ

Q: How do I get access to a system?
Submit an access request in the IT Service Desk portal. Your line manager must approve it first. The IT Security team then grants access within 3 working days.

Q: What are the password rules?
Passwords must have at least 12 characters. They must be changed every 90 days. You cannot reuse your last 5 passwords. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all staff accounts.

Q: What if I forget my password?
Use the self-service reset page. If that fails, call the IT Service Desk on extension 4100. The desk is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 6:00 pm.

Q: What happens when I lose my laptop?
Report it to the IT Service Desk within 2 hours. IT will lock the device remotely. Also lodge a police report within 24 hours.

Accounts that are unused for 60 days are disabled automatically. Staff who leave the company lose access on their last working day at 5:00 pm.
