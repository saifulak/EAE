Title: New Staff Onboarding FAQ

Q: What should I do on my first day?
Arrive at 9:00 am at the Level 1 reception. HR will give you an access card and laptop. The orientation session runs from 9:30 am to 12:30 pm.

Q: What documents do I bring?
Bring your MyKad or passport, a copy of your highest qualification, your bank account details, and your EPF and SOCSO numbers if you have them. Submit these to HR within the first 5 working days.

Q: Who helps me settle in?
Every new staff member gets a buddy for the first 60 days. The buddy is chosen by your line manager before you start.

Q: When is probation reviewed?
Probation lasts 3 months. Your manager holds check-ins at week 4 and week 8, and a final review in week 12.

Q: Which courses are compulsory?
Data handling, workplace safety, and the code of conduct. All three must be completed within 30 days of joining.
