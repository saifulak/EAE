Title: Remote Work Policy

Staff who have completed their 3-month probation may work remotely up to 2 days per week. The days must be agreed with the line manager and recorded in the team calendar by Friday of the previous week.

Core hours are 10:00 am to 4:00 pm Malaysia time. Remote staff must be reachable during core hours. Team meetings are normally held on office days.

Equipment: The company lends one laptop. Staff must use the company VPN for all work systems. Public Wi-Fi without VPN is not allowed. A monthly internet allowance of RM80 is paid to staff who work remotely at least 4 days a month.

Remote work from outside Malaysia is not allowed without written approval from the HR Director. Approval requests must be sent at least 30 days in advance.

Managers review remote arrangements every 6 months. The arrangement can be withdrawn with 14 days' notice if performance or security rules are not met.
