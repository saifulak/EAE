Title: Fire and Evacuation Drill Procedure

Seri Maju Berhad runs a fire evacuation drill twice a year, in March and September. Staff are told 1 week ahead of the date, but the exact time is not announced.

When the alarm sounds:
1. Stop work and leave your belongings.
2. Use the nearest stairs. Do not use lifts.
3. Go to your assembly point. The main assembly point is Car Park B, near the guard house.
4. Wait for your floor warden to count your team.

Each floor has 2 trained floor wardens. They wear green vests and must confirm that their floor is empty. The Safety Officer (a role, not a named person) signs off the drill report within 3 working days.

The target is to clear the building in under 8 minutes. If a drill takes longer than 10 minutes, a repeat drill is held within 30 days.

Staff who need help to evacuate should register with the Safety Officer so a buddy can be assigned.
