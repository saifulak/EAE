Title: Vendor Onboarding Procedure

Before Seri Maju Berhad buys from a new vendor, the vendor must be registered by the Procurement team. The business owner starts the process by submitting a Vendor Request Form.

Required documents: company registration certificate, bank letter, tax registration number, and a signed code of conduct. Vendors handling personal data must also sign a Data Processing Agreement.

Procurement completes due diligence checks within 10 working days after receiving all documents. The Head of Procurement (a role, not a named person) approves new vendors.

Purchases up to RM10,000 need 1 quotation. Purchases from RM10,001 to RM100,000 need 3 quotations. Purchases above RM100,000 go to a tender and need Management Committee approval.

Standard payment terms are 45 days from the invoice date. Vendor details are reviewed every year, and vendors with no orders for 24 months are marked inactive.
