Title: Overtime Policy

Overtime applies to non-executive staff whose monthly basic salary is RM3,600 or below. Executives are not paid overtime.

Overtime must be approved in writing by the line manager before the work starts. Unapproved overtime is not paid.

Pay rates, based on the hourly rate:
- Normal working day: 1.25 times.
- Rest day: 1.75 times.
- Public holiday: 2.5 times.

Staff may work at most 60 overtime hours in a month. Staff must have at least 10 hours of rest between two working shifts.

Overtime is recorded in the Timesheet system by the end of each week. Payroll processes overtime on the 25th of each month, together with the salary.

Instead of payment, a staff member may ask for time off in lieu. This needs both manager and HR approval, and must be taken within 60 days.
